Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Smart ATS CV (smartatscv.com). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to safeguard your data.

1. Information You Provide (Resume Data)

Smart ATS CV is built as a client-side tool. Any personal information you enter into the resume builder (such as your name, contact details, work history, and education) is processed locally in your browser. We do not store, collect, or share your resume content on our servers.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like many standard websites, smartatscv.com may make use of log files. These files merely log visitors to the site—usually a standard procedure for hosting companies and hosting analytics. The information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to smartatscv.com and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Smart ATS CV's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.
